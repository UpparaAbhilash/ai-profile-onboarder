Privacy and your data

This page explains which personal data this application stores, why, who can see it, how long it is kept, and how you can download or delete it.

What we store and why

  • Your resume (PDF): to create your profile. Stored under a random file name and never publicly accessible.
  • Profile details (name, contact details, experience, skills, projects): so reviewers can match you with jobs.
  • Date of birth and Aadhaar: only if they are in your resume or you add them. We store the last 4 digits of an Aadhaar number, never the full number, and remove it before any text is sent to the AI.
  • Job applications: so you and reviewers can follow their status.

AI processing

The text of your resume is sent to an AI service (OpenAI) to extract your profile details. Aadhaar numbers are removed first. Every value the AI returns is checked against your resume, and anything not found in it is discarded.

Who can see your data

  • You, after logging in.
  • Reviewers with explicit permission. Every time a reviewer opens your profile, this is recorded in an audit log.
  • Reviewer exports (CSV) never contain your contact details, date of birth or Aadhaar.
